# Cross-functional partner

## Who this is for

You work with this team from product, analytics, finance, or leadership. This path explains what the team does, how to ask it for work, and how to read what it produces.

## By end of week one

1. [Handbook home](../../index.md): how this reference is organized.
2. *Add: the economics page for the core product.* Why: everything the team does is judged against this.
3. *Add: the platform overview.* Why: where the data and compute live.

## By end of month one

1. *Add: the KPI pages the team is asked about most.* Why: these are the words the business uses.
2. *Add: the causal map for the primary outcome.* Why: it is the team's shared belief about what moves the number.
3. *Add: how we work, planning and review.* Why: how work enters and leaves the team.

## By end of quarter one

1. *Add: the model-family pages and their lessons.* Why: do not relearn what the team already paid for.
2. *Add: the decision records.* Why: the reasons behind the current shape of things.
