# New machine learning engineer

## Who this is for

You are joining as a machine learning engineer. By the end of quarter one you should be able to take a model from a notebook to a monitored production system on this platform without asking where anything is.

## By end of week one

1. [Handbook home](../../index.md): how this reference is organized.
2. *Add: the economics page for the core product.* Why: everything the team does is judged against this.
3. *Add: the data domain overview for the core product.* Why: the tables everything else is built on, and how to join them.
4. *Add: the platform overview.* Why: where the data and compute live.

## By end of month one

1. *Add: the KPI pages the team is asked about most.* Why: these are the words the business uses.
2. *Add: the causal map for the primary outcome.* Why: it is the team's shared belief about what moves the number.
3. *Add: how we work, planning and review.* Why: how work enters and leaves the team.

## By end of quarter one

1. *Add: the method pages and their lessons.* Why: do not relearn what the team already paid for.
2. *Add: two or three worked examples.* Why: how a question becomes a method, a result, and a measured outcome here.
3. *Add: the decision records.* Why: the reasons behind the current shape of things.
