# Onboarding

## Who this is for

You are joining the team as a data scientist, machine learning engineer, or
software engineer. By the end of quarter one you should be able to scope a piece
of work against a business outcome, find and trust the data for it, and take it
to production on this platform.

## By end of week one

1. [Handbook home](../index.md): how this reference is organized.
2. *Add: how the core product makes money.* Why: everything the team does is judged against this.
3. *Add: the core data domain.* Why: the tables everything else is built on, and how to join them.
4. *Add: the platform overview and development tools.* Why: where the data and compute live.

## By end of month one

1. *Add: the KPI pages the team is asked about most.* Why: these are the words the business uses.
2. [Development practices](../methods/development/index.md): how the team writes and reviews code.
3. [How we work](how-we-work.md): how work enters and leaves the team.

## By end of quarter one

1. *Add: the method pages and their lessons.* Why: do not relearn what the team already paid for.
2. [Oversight](../oversight/index.md): what is watched, who governs it, and the
   insights that have changed how the data should be read.
3. [Decision records](decisions/index.md): the reasons behind the current shape of things.

## Changing this handbook

By now you will have found a gap. Every page has an owner and a review date in
its front matter, so start by asking the owner. Facts that change live in one
place and are rendered into pages, so a number appears once; add the fact, not
the number. The rules are in the repository's `HANDBOOK-SPEC.md`.
