# Strategy

!!! note "Gather first"

    The current strategy deck, the OKR set or annual priorities, and any
    board or all-hands material that states where the company is going. The
    priorities themselves go in `facts/business.yaml` with their period,
    owner, and source; this page says what they mean for the team.

Where the company is trying to go and what it has said it will do first. A
lens page: judgment, not facts, and reviewed every half year.

## Stated priorities

*Add: the priorities for the current period, rendered from
`facts/business.yaml`, with one sentence each on what the business means by
it and which outcome it is meant to move.* Why: these are the words the
business uses to decide what the team works on.

## Where the company is going

*Add: the growth vectors as the business states them: existing products to
existing customers, new products, new markets, or acquisitions. A two-by-two
of product against market is a fine structure if it helps.* Why: a model built
for a market the company is leaving is a loop that will be retired.

## What the strategy asks of the team

*Add: the two or three things the strategy needs from data science, in the
business's words, and which rows of the [decision inventory](decisions.md)
they touch.* Why: this is the section that turns strategy into the team's
queue.

## What we would need to believe to change this

*Add: the conditions under which this page would be wrong: a priority dropped,
a market exited, a leadership change.* Why: a lens page rots quietly; this
section says what to watch for.
