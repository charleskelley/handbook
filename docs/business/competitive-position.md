# Competitive position

!!! note "Gather first"

    The competitor list from `facts/business.yaml`, win and loss notes if the
    business keeps them, pricing comparisons, and the competition section of
    the annual report or filing. Judgment about strengths and weaknesses is
    the page owner's and is dated by `last_reviewed`.

Who the company competes with, on what, and how it differs. One page for the
competitor list and the strengths and weaknesses against it.

## Competitors

*Add: a table rendered from `facts/business.yaml`: competitor, the segments
or customers where they compete, and the source.* Why: the list is a fact and
changes; keep it where facts live.

## How we differ

*Add: the company's strengths and weaknesses against the named competitors,
in the business's words, with the evidence for each.* Why: a differentiator
is a hypothesis about what moves an outcome, and the team can test it.

## Where competition shows up in the data

*Add: the datasets and KPIs where a competitor's move would be visible first:
churn by segment, win rate, price realisation.* Why: this is where competitive
position becomes a measurement question.

## What this means for the team

*Add: the outcomes competition pressures most, and the decisions in the
[decision inventory](decisions.md) that respond to it.* Why: the page earns
its place only by changing what the team watches or builds.

## What we would need to believe to change this

*Add: the conditions under which this page would be wrong: a new entrant, a
merger, a price move.* Why: a lens page rots quietly; this section says what
to watch for.
