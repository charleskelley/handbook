# Operating environment

!!! note "Gather first"

    Industry reports, regulator publications, and the industry section of
    the annual report or filing. Cite each under `sources`. Do not fill a
    force or factor the sources do not support; leave it out or mark it
    `*[unverified: needs source]*`.

The industry the company operates in and the external forces on it. One page
for both, because both are environment scans and a DS team needs them only
where they show up in an outcome.

## Industry structure

*Add: one short paragraph per force: rivalry among competitors, the threat of
new entrants, the threat of substitutes, the power of buyers, and the power of
suppliers. Say which forces actually bind this business and skip the ones that
do not.* Why: the binding forces decide which outcomes the business can move
and which are set by the market.

## External forces

*Add: the political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal
factors with a plausible effect on an outcome, one line each, and nothing
that does not.* Why: a complete scan is a consulting deliverable; a short one
is a reading aid.

## Trends to watch

*Add: the handful of trends that would show up in the data if they happened,
and which datasets or KPIs would show them. Link to
[Insights](../oversight/insights/index.md) for any that already have.* Why: this is
where the operating environment becomes a monitoring question.

## What this means for the team

*Add: the outcomes the environment constrains, and the decisions in the
[decision inventory](decisions.md) most exposed to it.* Why: the page earns
its place only by changing what the team watches or builds.
