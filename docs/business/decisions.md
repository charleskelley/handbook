# Decision inventory

!!! note "Gather first"

    Operating review decks, the process owners for each business area, the
    rules and models already in production, and the KPI pages. A row needs an
    owner who confirmed it; a decision nobody owns is listed with
    `*[unverified: needs source]*` in that column.

The decisions the business makes over and over, each of which is a candidate
outcome loop. The landscape's entry condition is a decision the business has
agreed to make, and this page is where those decisions are named.

## How to read this page

*Add: what counts as a row (a decision made repeatedly, with a recognisable
trigger and an action), what does not (one-off strategic choices, which are
[decision records](../team/decisions/index.md)), and any local rule for
ordering the table.* Why: the page is only useful if two people adding rows
draw the line in the same place.

The columns:

Decision
:   What is decided, as a question with an action attached.

Who decides
:   The role that makes or signs off the decision today.

Cadence
:   How often, or on what trigger.

Inputs
:   What the decider looks at, including any score or report.

Outcome moved
:   The business result the decision is meant to change, in the business's
    words.

Mechanism
:   One of judgment, rule, or model: how the decision is made today.

Loop
:   Open or closed. Closed means the decision is recorded, the outcome is
    measured against a counterfactual, and the result changes what runs next.

KPI
:   The KPI page that measures the outcome, if one exists.

## Candidates for an outcome loop

*Add: the open rows worth closing first, in order, with one sentence each on
why: size of the outcome, whether a counterfactual is feasible, whether the
decider wants it.* Why: this is the section the team plans from.

## How a row changes

*Add: who may add a row, who reviews it (the deciding role), and when a change
in mechanism, for example judgment to model, needs a decision record.* Why:
the inventory is a living list and the rules for changing it keep it true.
