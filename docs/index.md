# Handbook

This is the team's single reference for how we work: how the business makes
money and what it measures, the technology we run on, what we have learned
about modeling here, the conventions we follow, and how the team itself
operates. It is written for three readers at once: someone in their first
month, a team member looking something up, and a partner elsewhere in the
company trying to understand what we do and why.

## Start here

- New data scientist: [onboarding path](team/onboarding/data-scientist.md)
- New machine learning engineer: [onboarding path](team/onboarding/ml-engineer.md)
- Cross-functional partner: [onboarding path](team/onboarding/partner.md)

## Sections

- [Business](business/index.md): how the company and its products make money, the strategies that matter, the KPIs and what they mean.
- [Data](data/index.md): where the data comes from, how the warehouse is organized, what each dataset holds and how far to trust it.
- [Technology](technology/index.md): the platform, environments, local setup, and access.
- [Modeling](modeling/index.md): model families and their lessons, the features the business relies on, causal maps, postmortems.
- [Reference](reference/index.md): code style, conventions, best practices, recipes.
- [Team](team/index.md): how we work, onboarding, decision records.
- [Glossary](glossary/index.md): terms defined once.
- [Apps](apps/index.md): the registry of dashboards and companion applications.

## How this handbook is maintained

Every page has an owner and a review date in its front matter. Facts that
change live in one place and are rendered into pages, so a number appears once.
The rules are in the repository's `HANDBOOK-SPEC.md`.
